## GPS - Relativity Needed
GPS-system consists of 24 satellites with atomic clocks on board
Typically, a receiver gets timing signals from at least 4 satellites simulatenously
From the timing of these signals, receiver can determine its position
$$
|\vec{r}_{r} - \vec{r}_{i}| = c \cdot |t_{r}-t_{i}|, \space \text{for} \space r=1,\dots,4
$$
Time dilation effects must be accounted for
### Global Positioning System (GPS)
Correction due to time dilation:
$$
\frac{~1}{2}\left( \frac{V_{s}}{c} \right)^{2} \approx 0.84 \times 10^{-10}
$$
Correction due to gravitational redshift:
$$
\frac{GM_{\oplus}}{R_{s}c^{2}} \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-10}
$$
Military accuracy:
$$
2m \approx 6 ns \approx 1 \times 10^{-10}
$$
