## Why do we see "visible" light?
### Human-centric naming
### Universal biology
### One possible reason: the spectrum of the Sun
### The real reason - water transparency:
- Water is only transparent in a narrow frequency window
- Between vibrational and electronic resonances
- This window corresponds to "visible light"
- Since life evolved in water, vision evolved to use this transparent window

## What is a Field?
### Definition:
A field is a physical object that exists at any point of space and any time
### Examples:
- Temperature field
- Electromagnetic field

## EM Wave Properties
### Transverse Nature:
- $\vec{E}(\vec{x}, t)$ perpendicular to propagation direction
- Direction of $\vec{E}$ oscillation = polarization

### Magnetic Field:
- $\vec{B}(\vec{x}, t)$ perpendicular to both $\vec{E}$ and propagation
- In vacuum, $|\vec{B}|$ = $\frac{1}{c}|\vec{E}|$ for EM waves
- $\vec{E} \times \vec{B}$ defines propagation direction
