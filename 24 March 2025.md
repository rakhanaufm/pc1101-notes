
## Uncertainty in Diffraction
Diffraction by a slit with width $a$
Uncertainty in momentum
$$
\Delta p_{y} \geq p_{x}\theta_{1} \approx p_{x} \frac{\lambda}{a}
$$
Uncertainty in position
$$
\Delta y = a
$$
Photon momentum
$$
p_{x} \approx |\mathbf{p}| = \frac{h}{\lambda}
$$
Uncertainty in diffraction
$$
\Delta y\Delta p_{y} \geq h
$$
# Uncertainty Principle
In general, the uncertainty principle in quantum mechanics has the form 
$$
\Delta x\Delta p_{x} \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}
$$
Similarly, for time and energy,
$$
\Delta t\Delta E \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}
$$
